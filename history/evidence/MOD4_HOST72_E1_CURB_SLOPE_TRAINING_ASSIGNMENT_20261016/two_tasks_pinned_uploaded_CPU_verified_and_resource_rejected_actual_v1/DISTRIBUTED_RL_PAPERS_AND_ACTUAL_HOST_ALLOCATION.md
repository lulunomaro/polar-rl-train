- [DD-PPO, Wijmans et al., ICLR2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.00357): decentralized synchronous PPO across workers. This is a reference for eventually accelerating one shared policy; our installed stack supporting torchrun does not establish MOD4 distributed fullstate/RNG/support-normalization admission.

- [SEED RL, Espeholt et al., ICLR2020](https://research.google/pubs/seed-rl-scalable-and-efficient-deep-rl-with-accelerated-central-inference/): centralized accelerated inference and efficient transport. Our current simulation/inference already runs on GPU, so the reported speedups cannot be copied as a prediction for MOD4.
