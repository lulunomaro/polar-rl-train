## 3. CAN物理配置与DM实际命令链
- [CAN1](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/Src/can.c#L31-L106>)PD0/RX PD1/TX AF9；[CAN2](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/Src/can.c#L53-L137>)PB5/RX PB6/TX AF9。均NORMAL、prescaler3、SJW1、BS1=10/BS2=3：按42MHz PCLK1为1Mbit/s；RX0 IRQ priority6，自动busoff/重发均DISABLE。
- [filter](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/bsp/boards/bsp_can.c#L3-L23>)IDMASK全0接收、bank0/14→FIFO0、Start/notify；[IRQ](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/Src/stm32f4xx_it.c#L225-L275>)→HAL_CAN_IRQHandler→[callback](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/application/robot_cmd/CAN_receive.c#L334-L347>)→DecodeStdIdData。filter返回/初始化错误恢复未形成校验闭环。
- [SendCmd](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/application/chassis/chassis_balance.c#L1494-L1558>)→SendJointMotorCmd→DmMitCtrlTorque（普通运行）→[MitCtrl](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/application/robot_cmd/CAN_cmd_damiao.c#L161-L185>)→CAN_SendTxMessage→HAL_CAN_AddTxMessage；StandUp实际切MIT位置，SAFE切MIT零速。行为/起立算法另由model代理审，不在此复制。
- [DM commands](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/application/robot_cmd/CAN_cmd_damiao.c#L45-L145>)StdID=motor_id+mode：MIT=0/位置=0x100/速度=0x200；FF×7末字节FC enable/FD disable/FB clear/FE写零。当前motor初始化mode0，normal MIT并不走float位置/速度mode。
- [MIT codec](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/application/robot_cmd/motor.h#L134-L158>)P16bit±12.5rad/V12bit±30rad/s/Kp12bit0..500/Kd12bit0..5/T12bit±10名义Nm。[normal clamp](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/application/chassis/chassis_balance.c#L1269-L1294>)active ±7Nm；jump ±20支路已注释。[float_to_uint](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/components/algorithm/user_lib.c#L194-L217>)仅线性映射，无finite/限幅检查；不要把编码域当自动限扭。
- [feedback IDs](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/application/robot_cmd/CAN_receive.h#L55-L69>)DM 0x51..0x56（本机用51..54），LK141..144（用141/142）；[DM parse](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/application/robot_cmd/CAN_receive.c#L73-L87>)state高4bit/id低4bit，P16/V12/T12/MOS&rotor温度，标记HAL_GetTick接收到达。RX注释±45/±18不是实际宏±30/±10。
- GetMotorMeasure[实际读取](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/application/robot_cmd/CAN_receive.c#L426-L504>)按can/id−1取cache、超过10ms标offline；fdb数值尚未乘direction或ratio。无DLC/RTR/payload-id一致性检查、sample epoch/序号/同帧保证；arrival tick不是电机采样时间，反馈tor只是该drive协议名义量。

## 5. IMU真实路径与时序
- [IMU_task](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/application/IMU/IMU_task.c#L224-L266>)先Publish数据再delay7→BMI088自检/init失败100ms重试→IST8310 init循环→一次read/AHRS_init→SPI1 DMA。磁读更新虽注释，初始化IST循环仍active，别按头注释判断可省。
- [IMU IRQ](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/application/IMU/IMU_task.c#L627-L774>)PC4 accel/PC5 gyro DRDY→imu_cmd_spi_dma，gyro优先→SPI1 RX DMA2_Stream2完成→SW EXTI0→vTaskNotifyGiveFromISR→任务ulTaskNotifyTake→read_over/LPF→AHRS_update→get_angle/UpdateImuData。
- [SPI1](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/Src/spi.c#L91-L145>)PB3 SCK/PB4 MISO/PA7 MOSI，CS gyroPB0/accPA4；DMA2 RX S2/TX S3 channel3。[DMA IRQ](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/Src/dma.c#L53-L58>)priority5、EXTI4/9_5 priority5/EXTI0=6，按FreeRTOS syscall ceiling5配置。
- [BMI配置](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/components/devices/BMI088driver.c#L66-L115>)gyro2000°/s, ODR1kHz/116Hz带宽，accel3g/800Hz；[AHRS dt](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/application/IMU/IMU_task.c#L268-L332>)固定.001s，由gyro通知驱动、accel异步held，不是原生2kHz相干20样本包；二值flag/通知合并无公开drop/overrun/mean计数。
- [frame](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/application/IMU/IMU_task.c#L45-L49>)sensor→board=[raw_y,−raw_x,raw_z]，[量纲](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/components/devices/BMI088driver.h#L24-L46>)gyro系数.0010652644rad/s/count、accel.0008974359m/s²/count。[public fields](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/application/IMU/IMU_task.c#L388-L437>)Euler(rad)与body gyroXYZ、proper accel；pitch_vel=gyroY，不是任意roll下Euler导数。
- Quaternion/rawgyro/acc指针可读，但该public更新路径未赋epoch/seq/quality；AHRS_update实际来自已列AHRS.lib（本次不读/运行binary），IMU_solve.c虽编入且有EKF函数，selected application无其init/update caller，别宣称当前EKF活跃。
- 原C3输入需新的有界coherent packet/真实sample+arrival+aperture/age/overflow/installed-frame/lever/calibration绑定，不能用这些异步全局指针或1ms tick冒充已绑定100Hz/2kHz采样。当前仅识别可复用底层。

## 6. 失联→动作：可达与缺口
- [RC watchdog](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/application/other/remote_control.c#L573-L588>)direct DT7需receive_count>5且age<100ms；离线→[异常/模式](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/application/chassis/chassis_balance.c#L282-L335>)DBUS_ERROR→CHASSIS_SAFE。IMU仅判指针NULL，提前Publish不等于初始化/新鲜度已通过。
- [SAFE控制](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/application/chassis/chassis_balance.c#L1195-L1218>)joint设vel0；[SAFE send](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/application/chassis/chassis_balance.c#L1609-L1656>)MIT Kp0/Kd4（不是失能）、轮stop-PID值按torque发送。OFF才joint全0 MIT＋轮torque0，[DmMitStop](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/application/robot_cmd/CAN_cmd_damiao.c#L332-L337>)仍不是FD停使能/硬件断电。
- DmDisable/DmClearErr/ScanOfflineMotor在selected application检索仅定义；motor.offline和DM fault nibble未接ChassisHandleException；[detect defaults](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/application/assist/detect_task.c#L265-L312>)gyro2ms/accel5ms等诊断不等于控制停机，lost callback默认NULL（仅OLED例外）。
- Joint错误比较|feedback tor|>25，而实际合法12bit解码域±10，因此不把这一判断当有效过扭保护。DM本机超时/故障停止行为及电机固件参数未知，不能从0xD枚举推已执行停机。
- [transport](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/bsp/boards/bsp_can.c#L33-L45>)最多20次mailbox poll后无条件AddTx，忽略HAL返回、没有完成ACK/expiry/atomic6轴owner；[pacing](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/application/chassis/chassis_balance.c#L1504-L1526>)局部uint8_t cnt未初始化即%2，250us busywait依赖[SysTick](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/bsp/boards/bsp_delay.c#L7-L43>)。这是静态待复核点，不是已观测故障复现。
- [Error_Handler](<../DEPLOYED_BASELINE_AUDIT_V1/source/StandardRobotpp-balance1/Src/main.c#L225-L231>)为空；selected IOC/main没有IWDG/WWDG配置。未得到独立硬件watchdog/总线断线/调度冻结时完整停机证明；32bit HAL tick也不是新核心要求的64bit monotonic ns deadline provider。
