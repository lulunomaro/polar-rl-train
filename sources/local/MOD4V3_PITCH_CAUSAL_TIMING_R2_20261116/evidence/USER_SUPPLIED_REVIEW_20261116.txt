#### 1. 物理因果与优化目标打架：硬改 `-60` 预测惩罚治标不治本
看他自己跑的反事实回放 [causal_full/RESULT.json](file:///d:/claude2/fangzhen/_mod4v2_src_20261106/sources/local/MOD4V3_PITCH_CAUSAL_REPAIR_40M_20261115/evidence/causal_full/RESULT.json)：
